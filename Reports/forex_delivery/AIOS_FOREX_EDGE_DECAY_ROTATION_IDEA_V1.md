# AIOS Forex Edge Decay Rotation Idea V1

Status: IDEA_CAPTURED_ONLY
Date: 2026-10-08
Scope: AIOS Forex paper-first strategy rotation

## Core Idea

Replace manual weekly or monthly strategy tuning with an evidence-governed edge rotation loop.

The edge finder should keep producing and testing new bait. When a new setup passes the proof ledger, warm/cold scorer, edge autopilot, and paper campaign gates, it becomes eligible to replace or demote older strategies whose edge has decayed.

This should reduce the need to debug stale profit decay by turning strategy upkeep into a daily governed process.

## Safety Boundary

This design does not authorize live broker execution, live strategy installation, live compounding, or credential use.

Paper trading can be automated after repo approval. Live trading remains behind a separate explicit owner approval gate.

Old strategies should not be deleted. They should be retired, quarantined, and kept available for rollback and audit.

## Proposed Strategy States

| State | Meaning | Allowed Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CANDIDATE | New bait generated by hypothesis builder | Offline scoring only |
| WARM | Evidence is promising but not proven | Queue for proof expansion |
| HOT | Evidence passes core proof gates | Eligible for paper campaign |
| PAPER_ACTIVE | Strategy is installed in paper trading | Collect forward evidence |
| PROFITABLE_ACTIVE | Strategy is winning after costs in paper/live-approved mode | Continue with monitoring |
| WATCH_DECAY | Performance is weakening but not expired | Reduce confidence, increase scrutiny |
| STALE | Edge has decayed by rule | Stop new paper allocation and seek replacement |
| RETIRED | Strategy removed from active rotation | Preserve for audit and rollback |
| REPLACEMENT_READY | New edge has passed replacement gates | Eligible to replace stale paper strategy |

## Daily Rotation Loop

1. Run bait factory to generate candidate setups.
2. Score candidates with warm/cold rules.
3. Send HOT/WARM candidates into proof ledger expansion.
4. Promote only positive-positive edges with clean risk and cost-adjusted proof.
5. Compare active strategies against decay rules.
6. Mark weakening strategies as WATCH_DECAY or STALE.
7. Install replacements into paper rotation only after proof and paper readiness gates pass.
8. Keep retired strategy snapshots for rollback and postmortem.
9. Produce a daily ledger entry with every promotion, demotion, retirement, and blocked action.

## Initial Stale Edge Rules

A strategy can be marked WATCH_DECAY or STALE only after enough recent closed trades exist to avoid reacting to noise.

| Rule | WATCH_DECAY Trigger | STALE Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rolling expectancy after costs | Falls below +0.05R | Falls to 0R or below |
| Profit factor | Falls below 1.25 | Falls below 1.10 |
| Drawdown | Reaches 75% of configured cap | Breaches configured cap |
| Loss streak | Reaches 2 consecutive losses | Breaches the policy loss limit |
| Slippage/spread drift | Repeatedly worse than tested assumptions | Makes expected edge non-positive |
| Data freshness | Forward evidence older than 3 days | Forward evidence older than 7 days |
| Regime mismatch | Signal regime drifts from proven sample | Repeated mismatch with losing outcomes |

## Install Rules

A new strategy can replace an old paper strategy only when all are true:

- It is HOT or better after warm/cold scoring.
- It is recorded in the proof ledger.
- It has no broker, live, order, money, or compounding authority attached.
- It passes risk policy checks.
- It has a rollback snapshot.
- It has a paper campaign handoff package.

A live strategy can never be auto-installed from this process without a separate explicit owner approval.

## Compounding Rule

Compounding should remain disabled until the strategy has:

- Persistent positive expectancy after costs.
- Forward paper evidence across the required sample size.
- Drawdown inside the approved risk envelope.
- No unresolved false-positive flags.
- Owner approval for the exact live and compounding scope.

## Proposed Processing Hook

The future component should be named:

`forex_edge_decay_rotation_manager_v1`

It should consume:

- Bait factory output.
- Warm/cold scorer output.
- Proof ledger records.
- Edge autopilot state.
- Paper campaign results.

It should emit:

- Daily decay report.
- Strategy state changes.
- Paper install requests.
- Retirement records.
- Rollback metadata.
- Blocked live-action records when approval is missing.

## Decision

This idea is approved for design capture and future paper-first implementation planning.
It is not approved for live trading automation yet.
